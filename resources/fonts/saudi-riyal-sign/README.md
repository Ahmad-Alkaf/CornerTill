`Saudi_Riyal_Symbol-2.svg` is the official Saudi Riyal symbol of the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), downloaded unchanged on 2026-11-13 from https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-sa/Currency/Documents/Saudi_Riyal_Symbol-2.svg (linked as "SVG" on https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Currency/SRS/Pages/Guideline.aspx). The symbol is SAMA's. `saudi-riyal-sign.woff2` uses it unchanged in shape and proportion (uniform scale only), as the SAMA Saudi Riyal Symbol Guidelines require. `build.py` makes the font from the SVG.
